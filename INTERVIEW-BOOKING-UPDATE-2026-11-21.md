# Interview booking link

Moving an application to Interview sends a booking email with https://calendar.app.google/b2bKN2LUkKdZ4sQY7. Leave the scheduled invitation unpublished until the chosen date, time and interviewers are recorded. No date is required simply to move to Interview.

The applicant portal shows a booking button when at Interview without a published time. Existing published interviews retain confirmation and reminder behaviour. This update does not integrate Google Calendar APIs: the team must record bookings in Élan to activate accurate portal reminders. Use the same applicant name and email when booking.

The email functions and scripts/interview-booking-link.sql are deployed to elan-v2. Upload this complete website package through the existing GitHub deployment. No historical invitations were resent.
