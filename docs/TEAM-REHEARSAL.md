# Organiser sign-in, roster and rehearsal
After your GitHub-connected Cloudflare deployment succeeds, the public sign-in address is https://elansummit.com/organisers. The previous organiser-desk address redirects. The login is deliberately excluded from the public XML sitemap. Public 2026 committee, recruitment and cabinet decision pages are included. No Google Search Console submission is implied.

Admin → 2026 OC members → Add a member: enter name, department, committee role, About this member, portrait and display order. Save unchecked for a draft; select Publish and save to show the profile. This does not create login access.

For 2027 access, an organiser first creates and verifies their own account. In Admin → Committee & access assign that verified email a department and member/lead permission. Leadership approval seats need two distinct verified emails. Existing app sign-in is shared; nobody picks a role at login.

Human phone rehearsal (ideally on a staging project; avoid invented finance receipts):
1. Have a Social Media organiser propose a real draft, attach an image, note where they expect feedback, then close the browser.
2. Each leader independently signs in, opens Needs my decision, views the creative and records a decision. If deferring, assign the revisit date and owner. The other person's pending vote stays visible under Waiting for.
3. Ask the organiser to sign in again, find the decision and explain their next step without assistance. Note what they couldn't find. Nothing is published to social media automatically.
4. Open First edition on a phone, rotate, close the menu and use Back. Confirm the text remains visible.
5. For a separate sponsorship exercise, specify only instructions the receiving department may read. Both approvals create linked responsibilities; Finance records actual money independently.
6. For missing 2026 stories, enter short permission-cleared testimonials in Admin → Stories & film with attribution and role (student/delegate or teacher/faculty). Only approved, published testimonials appear. Supply confirmed names, portraits and bios to finish the roster.
