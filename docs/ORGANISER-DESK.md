# Organiser workspace
Deploy the contents of dist to the website repository. The two additive operations migrations are installed in Élan’s Supabase project. Existing assignments, account invitations and the two-person approval workflow are retained.

Leadership opens /organisers to a summit overview. Use Departments to see active organisers, heads and approved register entries, Work across teams to assign responsibilities with due dates, Briefings to publish departmental instructions, and Finance overview for balances from posted transactions. The approval inbox handles submissions and the handoff view shows commitments made to departments. Website editing, account management, appointments and recruitment remain in admin.

Department heads can assign tasks within their own team, update progress, review handoffs and submit work for leadership review. Members see their own tasks, department briefings and relevant working tools. Finance, social media, design, partnerships, outreach, logistics, programme, documentation and campus ambassadors each have a working register or ledger. New custom departments receive a register when an account is assigned. A workspace without records shows a next action instead of sample data.

Verified: local browser preview with an empty Assistant Director workspace; rollback database tests for leadership, heads and members; site build and references; existing automated tests. The production frontend updates after Cloudflare deploys dist.
