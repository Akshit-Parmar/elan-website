2026 directory: First edition → Organising committee. Admin → Committee & access lets you add a member, enter a department, upload a portrait and publish or unpublish a profile. Group order follows the earliest member display order. Without a portrait the card uses initials. A public profile never grants account access.

2027 recruitment: Take part → Organise Élan 2027. Submissions go through the existing stored enquiry workflow with kind volunteer and intent "2027 organising committee application". Find them in Organiser inbox by searching that phrase. Sending an application does not grant a role or send an automatic email.

Private workspace: Take part → Organiser desk. After selection, the organiser creates and verifies their account. In Admin → Committee & access, enter their email, department and permission. Assign a task with a brief. Members may read and update status on their own tasks in their assigned department. Leads can do the same for all tasks in their department. Admins manage all assignments. Editing an assignment replaces the current department/role. Revoke removes desk permissions without deleting historical tasks or changing other independently granted staff privileges. Do not grant legacy admin/staff roles to people who only need this desk.

The additive committee.sql migration is already installed on the existing Élan project. No database setup is required. Use the normal GitHub/Cloudflare deployment instructions. Only public committee and recruitment pages are included in the sitemap.

Organiser control panel: /organiser-desk is a standalone sign-in and workspace with Overview, Tasks, Briefings and Resources. The organiser's verified account determines its assigned department and permission. Department leads also have Post an update. In Admin → Committee & access, publish resources and briefings to members, leads or the entire named department. Content permissions are checked in the database. Resource links open at their destination; control access to externally hosted documents separately. The additional organiser-panel.sql migration is already installed.

Department tools (2027): Admin → Committee & access lists enabled department names. Assign a verified account to the matching department. Admins can enable another named department and choose the appropriate register template. No real role assignments or financial transactions are created during setup.

Finance: enter opening funds once; post a dated transaction with amount, type, party/reference and optional PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP bill (8 MB). Bills are private and downloaded only through authorised account requests. A failed bill upload can be retried on the saved transaction without reposting the amount. Previously posted transactions cannot be overwritten. A Finance lead may post a dated reversal and then a corrected entry. Choose expense paid for an immediate purchase, or unpaid bill followed by payment of existing payable; do not enter both as a fresh expense. Likewise, collection of a receivable is not a second sale. The panel presents available funds, receipts, spending, cumulative income/expenditure, an as-of balance sheet and CSV journal export. The numbers are internal management accounts in INR, based on manually classified entries. No bank sync, tax filing, automatic depreciation or automatic reading of bills. Do not treat documents at linked external URLs as protected by the website.

Outreach tracks schools, contacts, stage and next follow-up; Operations tracks arrangements, locations, suppliers and dates; Programme tracks session briefs, rooms, leads and schedules; Media tracks deliverables, owners, channels, deadlines and approvals; Partnerships tracks contacts, proposed contributions, follow-ups and commitments. Members see the shared register and edit their own entries; department leads can edit the whole register. A sponsorship pledge does not automatically post money received into Finance.

Current approval workflow (supersedes direct department posting above)
Social Media is a separate department and calendar. In Admin → Committee & access → Leadership approval seats, assign distinct verified accounts for Director and Assistant Director. No real seats are assigned by the release. Reviewers sign in through the organiser control panel; an OC department assignment is not required for a review-only account.

All new department register entries and edits, finance postings and reversals, additional bills, and department-lead briefings/resources enter the review queue. Finance requires a private PDF or photo (8 MB maximum), including for cash. Teams can attach a creative reference file and/or link to a register proposal. Review the uploaded version for a stable record; external links may change. Each department can track its submissions. Existing approved/legacy records are not retroactively changed. Ordinary assigned-task progress and administrator access management remain operational controls.

Two distinct accounts each record one decision per round on the same immutable submission. Two approvals apply the item atomically. After both vote, a rejection takes precedence; otherwise any Later decision holds it. A leader can reopen a deferred item; both votes are required again and previous rounds remain visible. A rejected proposal can be submitted anew with changes. Stale proposed changes never silently replace a newer register version. Previous finance uploads remain accessible and verified new evidence is linked to the posted transaction. Private evidence is available only within authorised review/department scope.
