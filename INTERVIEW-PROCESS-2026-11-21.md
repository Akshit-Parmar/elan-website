# Élan OC interview process

1. Review the application and department feedback. Move suitable applicants to Interview without publishing a scheduled invitation. This sends the booking link. Akshit can send the agreed personal WhatsApp introduction separately.
2. The applicant chooses a Google Calendar slot using the application email. Calendar is the booking source; Élan does not automatically import or change it.
3. The assigned review owner checks Calendar, matches the email, records the precise time, time zone, duration, interviewer(s) and joining instructions in Élan, then publishes. The portal confirmation checks that the copied details are correct. Existing scheduled reminders require this recorded, published booking.
4. Prepare from the application and department feedback. Explain the role, reporting line, unpaid nature, realistic time commitment and remote/on-site requirements. Discuss one concrete past example and one department scenario. Leave space for questions. Do not promise unconfirmed summit dates or venue.
5. Each interviewer saves their scorecard with evidence. Save the shared interview feedback and recommendation. A passed time is not proof of attendance: record an absence and contact the applicant where appropriate.
6. Leadership agrees the next step; the admin confirms final appointment and department access. The saved applicant-facing stage sends the automatic update. Private feedback does not email the applicant.

## Queues
- Booking not recorded: Interview stage without a published time. Check Calendar before following up.
- Upcoming interviews: published future time.
- Awaiting portal confirmation: future published time without a portal reply.
- Reschedule requests: applicant asked for another time; coordinate in Calendar and then update Élan.
- Interview follow-up: the recorded end time passed and the application is still at Interview. This does not mean the interview happened.

No new database migration is required for this process update. The existing booking emails remain live. Deploy the updated website through GitHub. Existing applicants have not been re-invited or bulk-messaged.
