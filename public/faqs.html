General

Élan is a premium student-led interschool event experience built around creativity, performance, leadership, school spirit, networking, community, and its two flagships, the crisis simulation Crisis Command and the business arena Cent$ible.

Approved students, school delegations, invited guests, educators, confirmed supporters and volunteers, media, and collaborators can attend according to the final registration rules.

Bring your registration confirmation, school ID if applicable, a charged phone, any required materials, and personal essentials shared in the final event guidelines.

Schedule changes will be communicated through official event channels, email, coordinator updates, and on-site announcements.

Crisis Command

Crisis Command is Élan’s flagship event. Teams act as the emergency governing council of a fictional country and respond to an evolving national crisis across both days.

No. It has public speaking and strategy, but it is not an MUN, debate, or conference. It is a live crisis simulation with roles, updates, teamwork, final response, and judging.

No. Students need curiosity, teamwork, and willingness to think under pressure. The format rewards leadership, communication, realism, ethics, strategy, and adaptability.

The ideal team structure is five students, with roles such as Head of State, Finance Minister, Foreign Affairs Minister, Policy Advisor, and Media & PR Head.

Yes. After the Day 1 briefing and strategy block, each team submits an Initial Response Brief. It records the team’s immediate priorities, resource choices, public communication approach, and first action plan before Day 2 consequences are introduced.

Cent$ible

Cent$ible is Élan’s business flagship, run in collaboration with Sanjay Ghodawat International School. It is a live inter-school business arena for Grades 9 to 12 where teams compete and collaborate across four themes, and the strongest teams advance to the Boardroom Finale.

Cent$ible is open to school students in Grades 9 to 12, across schools and delegations.

It is team-based. Cent$ible is built around four competitive themes, and each theme is its own competition with several rounds. The strongest teams from every theme go through to the Boardroom Finale to compete for the overall championship, and there are networking sessions and interactive activities between rounds.

The four themes are BRAND बनाओ, VIRAL व्यापर, NOTE नीति, and प्रश्न PRODIGY. Each theme runs its own rounds and sends its top teams to the Boardroom Finale.

Cents are Cent$ible’s shared in-event currency. Teams earn, invest, and bid with Cents as the rounds play out.

The Boardroom Finale is where the top teams from every theme come together to decide the overall Cent$ible champion.

No specific business background is required. Cent$ible is open to all students in Grades 9 to 12, and the rounds are built around creativity, strategy, adaptability, communication, and quick thinking.

On the Tickets / Registration page, select Cent$ible as your flagship and choose your theme in the registration form. Cent$ible is for Grades 9 to 12.

Venue

Élan is planned at SGI Pune Campus, Pune, Maharashtra. Venue access, reporting gates, and room allocations will be shared with confirmed students and school coordinators.
