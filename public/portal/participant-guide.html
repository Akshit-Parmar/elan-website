Participant Guide.

Everything you need before and during Élan Summit.

What to bring

A valid photo ID, your ticket (this portal), and personal essentials. Bring formals for the opening and closing ceremonies.

Dress code

Formals for the opening and closing ceremonies. For the rest of the event, wear anything comfortable and decent.

Reporting

Reporting and briefing times are on the Schedule. Report to the Élan registration desk on arrival to collect your pass.

ID requirements

Carry a school or government photo ID that matches the details on your registration.

Code of conduct

Treat every participant, teacher, guest, and organiser with respect. Follow the Élan team's instructions, stay with your delegation, and help keep the venue clean. Serious misconduct may lead to removal from the event without a refund.

Food

Food will be provided during the event. Meal breaks are marked on the Schedule.

Accommodation

Accommodation for the night of 5 September is included with the Early Bird, Standard and Late Registration passes. Day Delegate passes do not include accommodation or transport — those participants make their own arrangements. Full logistics will be shared closer to the event.

Not allowed

No alcohol, smoking or vaping, illegal substances, weapons, or hazardous items. Do not leave the venue without permission from the Élan team.

Emergency & rules

For any emergency during the event, call the Élan team on +91 8160215010. Keep your ID and pass on you at all times, stay with your delegation, and follow all venue safety instructions.
