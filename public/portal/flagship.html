The main event

Crisis Command.

Élan's flagship two-day leadership simulation. Crisis Command and Cent$ible run at the same time — if you chose Crisis Command at registration, this is your flagship.

Overview

Lead under pressure.

Mixed-school teams step into the emergency council of a fictional nation and respond to an evolving national crisis under real time pressure across both days of Élan.

Team structure

Cabinet-style roles.

  • Head of State
  • Finance Minister
  • Foreign Affairs Minister
  • Policy Advisor
  • Media & PR Head
Evaluation

Judged on real skills.

  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Strategy and realism
  • Ethics and adaptability
  • Communication under pressure

Initial plan submission

On Day 1, after the initial briefing, teams must submit their Initial Response Brief by the end of the briefing window, or as per the announced deadline. Watch Announcements for the exact cutoff.

Submission status: not submitted

Final presentation

On Day 2, teams manage the consequences of their Day 1 decisions, finalise their response, and present and defend it before a panel. Format and timings are on the Schedule.
