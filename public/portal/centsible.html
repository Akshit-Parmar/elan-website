The Boardroom Finale

The three highest-scoring teams from every theme advance to the Boardroom Finale on Day 2, the round that decides the overall Cent$ible championship. In प्रश्न PRODIGY this stage is called the Nova Boardroom.

The Boardroom is a business decision-making arena rather than a quiz or a pitch. You are given a business situation drawn from startup strategy, marketing, finance, brand management, business expansion, crisis management or entrepreneurial decision-making. Analyse the information, identify the opportunity, decide on a course of action, then justify that decision and answer board questioning.

Judged on business knowledge, strategic thinking, creativity, decision-making, problem-solving, financial understanding, communication, confidence, teamwork and composure under pressure. Watch Announcements for your theme's round times and reporting details.
