Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Élan handles inquiry, registration, school, volunteer, and contact information submitted through the website.

Information collected

Élan may collect information submitted through registration, contact, school, and volunteer forms, including name, email, phone, school, organization, ticket type, and message details.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, process registration interest, coordinate delegations, communicate event updates, and manage event operations.

Data sharing

Élan does not sell submitted information. Information may be shared only with relevant event coordinators or service providers required to operate the event.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the organizer using the official contact details on this website.
