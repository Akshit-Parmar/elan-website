The main events

Crisis Command and Cent$ible sit at the centre of Élan.

The event is not only performances and networking. Its main competitive experiences are its two flagships. Crisis Command is a two-day crisis leadership simulation where mixed-school teams lead a fictional nation through briefing, Day 1 initial response submission, escalation, final response, and presentation. Cent$ible is an inter-school business arena where teams compete across four themes and the strongest reach the Boardroom Finale. The two flagships run at the same time, so each participant takes part in one.

01

Real roles

Head of State, Finance Minister, Foreign Affairs Minister, Policy Advisor, and Media & PR Head.

02

Live escalation

The crisis changes across the event, forcing students to adapt instead of relying on prepared answers.

03

Final response

Teams first submit an Initial Response Brief on Day 1, then submit and defend a final strategic response on Day 2. Judging focuses on leadership, realism, ethics, communication, and teamwork.
