How it works

One arena, four themes, one champion.

Cent$ible is built around four business themes. Each one is its own competition with several rounds, and the strongest teams from every theme go through to the Boardroom Finale to fight for the overall championship.

Format

Team-based

You compete in teams in the theme you choose. Between the rounds there are networking sessions and interactive activities.

Eligibility

Ages 14 to 19

Open to school students aged 14 to 19, across schools and delegations. First-year university students within the same age range are also eligible and can register individually.

Currency

Cents

A shared in-event currency. Teams earn it, invest it, and bid with it as the rounds play out.

The finale

Boardroom Finale

The top teams from each theme meet in the finale to decide the overall champion.

Theme 1

BRAND बनाओ.

Build, adapt, pitch, and dominate. BRAND बनाओ is where you create a real startup and pitch it under pressure, with your idea evolving across three rounds the way it draws on Shark Tank, a startup expo, and live business decisions.

Before the event

Your Product Brief

Two days before Élan, every team submits a short product information form: brand name, product or service, target customer, price range, business model, sales channel, key differentiator, one major dependency and your expansion idea. This is what the organising team uses to build a crisis tailored to your startup. You will not be told what that crisis is.

Round 1

The Founder Pitch

A five-minute pitch, delivered as founders speaking to investors. Cover your brand name and what it means, your logo and the thinking behind it, your target audience, your business model and how you make money, your marketing strategy, and your future vision. Judges may stop you at five minutes, so rehearse to the clock.

Round 2

Brand Under Pressure

After lunch you receive a sealed envelope containing a crisis built specifically for your startup. You get twenty minutes to analyse it and rebuild your strategy, then three minutes to present your recovery plan: what happened, what you do first, what changes, how you protect customer trust, and how you prevent it happening again. The top three teams go through to the Boardroom.

Skills tested: Adaptability, Business Intelligence, Public Speaking, Creativity, Critical Thinking, and Crisis Management.

Theme 2

VIRAL व्यापार.

Sell the impossible and own the market. VIRAL व्यापार is a live branding and advertising battle where teams build brands, run campaigns, survive PR disasters, and win over a live audience, the way a real agency does when things go sideways.

Round 1

The Comeback Pitch

You are handed a real brand that failed, not because the product was bad, but because the marketing was. Your sheet gives you its history, why it failed and where it stands now. In thirty minutes work out what went wrong, what the brand should become, who it should target and how you would make it matter again, then pitch the revival in five minutes. A slide deck or a reference picture of the product is required.

Round 2

Unexpected Collab

Two fish bowls. You draw one brand from the first and one product category from the second, and you have to make that collaboration believable. Nike and furniture. Crocs and luggage. Thirty minutes to prepare, five to pitch: what the collaboration is, why these two belong together, what exactly you are launching, who it is for, what makes it different, and the campaign idea behind it.

Qualification

Top three to the Boardroom

Judging weights creativity and originality, marketing strategy, understanding of the challenge, execution and presentation, audience engagement and time management across both rounds. The strongest three teams advance to the Cent$ible Boardroom on Day 2.

Skills tested: Branding, Ad Creation, Public Speaking, Sales Skills, Audience Impact, and Crisis Handling.

Theme 3

NOTE नीति.

Risk everything and survive the chaos. NOTE नीति is a live financial simulation where you trade, negotiate, ride out crashes, and make risky calls under pressure, working with real market situations instead of textbook problems.

Round 1

The Money Market Mayhem

Two hours on a live market run through the Élan portal. You invest your Cents across companies that rise and fall, and every five minutes the host announces a new market status: you hold, sell or switch. Your portfolio is valued live, the leaderboard moves with it, and when the final freeze is called your closing capital is locked.

Round 2

The Coin Economy

The floor becomes physical. Every team is handed thirty coins, ten red, ten blue and ten black, and five zones open at once: Dice of Fortune, the Spin Wheel, Stock Market Dart, Investment Plinko and the Bull versus Bear Arena. You choose your own route, you may play any zone twice, and your team can split up to cover more ground. Ninety minutes, then the zones close and your coins are counted.

Qualification

Top three to the Boardroom

Your Round 1 closing capital is added to the value of the coins you finish with. The three teams holding the most combined capital go through to the Cent$ible Boardroom on Day 2.

Skills tested: Strategy, Risk Management, Quick Thinking, Adaptability, Financial Awareness, and Decision-Making.

Theme 4

प्रश्न PRODIGY.

Think fast, analyse smart, and win big. This is not a normal quiz. प्रश्न PRODIGY is a fast business battle where teams crack real problems, recognise brands on sight, and bid against each other, sitting somewhere between Kaun Banega Crorepati, Shark Tank, and a live auction.

Round 1

Rapid Vision Rush

One minute per team, as many questions as you can take. Logos, taglines, founders, emoji brand clues, product silhouettes, advertisement visuals and current business affairs, deliberately shuffled so you can never settle into one kind of thinking. Every correct answer is worth 100 Cents, there is no negative marking, and you may skip. Nobody is eliminated, and what you earn here you carry into the next round.

Round 2A

Rapid Fire Rush

Answer as many questions as you can before the clock runs out, covering brands, taglines, CEOs, startups, products, and current affairs. Every correct answer is worth 100 Cents, and there is no negative marking.

Round 2B

Visionary Quest

This time the clues are visual: brand logos, emoji clues, product silhouettes, startup icons, and ad clips. Every correct answer is worth 100 Cents, and the leaderboard can flip fast.

Round 2

The Bidding War

You bid your Cents for the right to answer a question you have not seen. Bidding opens at 100 Cents and teams bid each other up. Answer correctly and your full bid is added to your score. Get it wrong and half your bid is taken away. The skill is knowing when the reward justifies the risk. The top three teams after this round go through to the Nova Boardroom.

Skills tested: Business Knowledge, Confidence, Strategic Bidding, Risk-Taking, and Pressure Handling.
