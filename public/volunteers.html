Why volunteer

Real responsibility. Real experience.

Volunteers learn how to manage people, stages, guests, schedules, media, logistics, and pressure inside a live event environment.

01

Leadership exposure

Handle real event responsibilities and learn to make decisions quickly.

02

Certificate and recognition

Receive acknowledgement for service, commitment, and contribution.

03

Network building

Work with students, coordinators, guests, supporters, and event teams.

08

Crisis Command support

Help manage briefing rooms, team movement, submission flow, finalist coordination, and backstage pressure during the flagship simulation.

09

Cent$ible support

Help run the business themes: round timing, scoring flow, Cents tracking, and Boardroom Finale coordination during the flagship arena.

Roles

Volunteer roles.

Each role should have a coordinator, reporting time, briefing, and clear checklist before the event day.

01

Registration

Check-in, verification, badges, queues, and arrival support.

02

Hospitality

Guest comfort, green room coordination, refreshments, and welcome support.

03

Stage support

Mic handling, cue sheets, performer movement, and backstage discipline.

04

Audience management

Seating, movement, crowd guidance, and safety coordination.

05

Speaker support

Guest arrival, session readiness, green room, and communication.

06

Media/content

Photo, video, reels, interviews, captions, and live content capture.

07

Logistics

Supplies, signage, movement, stall support, and problem-solving.
