The main event

Crisis Command: the main reason Élan feels different.

Two days. One crisis. No easy answers. Crisis Command is Élan’s signature leadership simulation where mixed-school teams step into the emergency council of a fictional nation and respond to an evolving national crisis.

Crisis Command

Lead under pressure.

Students take on cabinet-style roles, receive crisis updates, build a response, submit a Day 1 Initial Response Brief, manage public trust, negotiate priorities, and defend final decisions in front of a panel.

Explore Crisis CommandSee Day 1 & Day 2 Flow

  • Head of State
  • Finance Minister
  • Foreign Affairs Minister
  • Policy Advisor
  • Media & PR Head

Full crisis flow

Judged on real skills

  • Strategy and realism
  • Ethics and adaptability
  • Communication under pressure
