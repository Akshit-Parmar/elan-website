School collaboration with Élan.

Most schools will participate as delegations. A small number may collaborate more closely with Élan, but collaboration is not just a logo exchange. It requires shared responsibility, clear coordination, and active contribution to the event experience.

Collaboration model

What we expect from collaborating schools.

A collaborating school gets stronger visibility and ownership, but also takes on meaningful responsibility. The aim is to protect Élan’s premium standard while giving the collaborating school a real leadership role.

Coordination ownership

Appoint a responsible school coordinator, maintain communication with the Élan team, and support planning conversations before the event.

Student participation

Send an active delegation that participates seriously across Crisis Command, Cent$ible, performances, networking blocks, and selected event moments.

Operational support

Depending on the collaboration level, assist with outreach, logistics, faculty coordination, venue support, registration flow, or student management.

Promotion support

Help share approved event communication with students, parents, school networks, and relevant student communities.

Discipline and safety

Support student conduct, attendance, timing, supervision, and emergency communication standards throughout the experience.

Brand alignment

Respect the Élan identity, communication tone, timelines, and final event decisions so the event feels consistent and premium.

Benefits

What collaborators gain.

Collaboration is useful only when both sides benefit. A school that contributes meaningfully can receive stronger recognition, visibility, student leadership opportunities, and association with a premium event.

01

Stronger school visibility

Collaborating schools can receive approved recognition on the website, event communication, stage mentions, and selected media assets.

02

Leadership positioning

The school is seen as an active supporter of interschool leadership, creativity, student voice, and experiential learning.

03

Student ownership

Students from the collaborating school may gain deeper exposure through coordination roles, media moments, delegation leadership, or hosted segments.

04

Network access

Collaboration creates stronger relationships with participating schools, guests, educators, and youth communities.

05

Premium association

The school becomes associated with a modern event identity, not a basic school competition or notice-board activity.

06

Clear credit

Any collaboration credit is defined in advance so expectations, responsibilities, and recognition remain fair and transparent.

Important note

Participation is simpler.

A school does not need to collaborate in order to attend. Regular delegation participation is the recommended path for most schools.

Collaboration is selective

Responsibility first.

Élan will consider collaboration only when the school is ready to contribute reliably and protect the event standard.

Next step

Start with a school inquiry.

Schools interested in either participation or collaboration should begin through the School Delegations form.
