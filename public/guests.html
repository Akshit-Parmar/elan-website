Guest Experience

Not just invited. Properly hosted.

Guests should feel that Élan is organised, respectful, and polished without feeling amateur. This section makes that promise clear.

01

Warm arrival

Clear contact person, smooth entry, reserved seating, and a hosted welcome so guests do not have to figure things out on their own.

02

Stage clarity

Guests receive their exact moment, expected role, time slot, and event context before they arrive.

03

Media-ready

Photos, short clips, gratitude posts, and clean event documentation can be prepared for confirmed guests.

04

Student interaction

Selected moments can include Q&A, judging feedback, mentor circles, or informal networking with participants.

05

Respectful timing

The guest schedule should stay sharp, realistic, and respectful of their time.

06

Proper recognition

Every confirmed guest should be acknowledged with a stage mention, digital credit, and a formal thank-you.

For invited guests

Confirmed guests will receive a briefing note, schedule slot, venue details, arrival contact, and the exact role they are expected to play at Élan.
