The complete Élan event overview.

Everything schools, students, parents, guests, and volunteers need to understand the Élan experience, including dates, venue, safety, registration, and the flagship events, Crisis Command and Cent$ible.

Date

5–6 September 2026 · A focused two-day weekend experience with arrival, stage sessions, competitions, networking, cultural energy, and recognition.

Venue

SGIS Pune Campus, Mulshi · Main reporting will be coordinated through the Élan registration desk, with stage, delegation, supporter, and volunteer zones clearly marked.

City

Pune, Maharashtra · A premium student event setting with school-friendly coordination and access planning for local and visiting delegations.

The main events

Crisis Command and Cent$ible sit at the centre of Élan.

The event is not only performances and networking. Its main competitive experiences are its two flagships. Crisis Command is a two-day crisis leadership simulation where mixed-school teams lead a fictional nation through briefing, Day 1 initial response submission, escalation, final response, and presentation. Cent$ible is an inter-school business arena where teams compete across two themes and the strongest reach the Boardroom Finale. The two flagships run at the same time, so each participant takes part in one.

01

Real roles

Head of State, Finance Minister, and Foreign Affairs Minister.

02

Live escalation

The crisis changes across the event, forcing students to adapt instead of relying on prepared answers.

03

Final response

Teams first submit an Initial Response Brief on Day 1, then submit and defend a final strategic response on Day 2. Judging focuses on leadership, realism, ethics, communication, and teamwork.

Overview

A premium interschool experience.

Élan combines creativity, performance, leadership, school spirit, networking, guest sessions, competitions, volunteer coordination, and flagship moments into one polished event experience.

The event is open to approved students, school delegations, guests, volunteers, confirmed supporters, educators, and invited collaborators according to final registration guidelines.

Who should attend?

  • Schools looking to send student delegations.
