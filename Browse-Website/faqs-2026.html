General

Élan is a premium interschool event experience built around creativity, performance, leadership, school spirit, networking, community, and its two flagships, the crisis simulation Crisis Command and the business arena Cent$ible.

Approved students, school delegations, invited guests, educators, confirmed supporters and volunteers, media, and collaborators can attend according to the final registration rules.

Schedule changes will be communicated through official event channels, email, coordinator updates, and on-site announcements.

Crisis Command

Crisis Command is Élan’s flagship event. Teams act as the emergency governing council of a fictional country and respond to an evolving national crisis across both days.

No. It has public speaking and strategy, but it is not an MUN, debate, or conference. It is a live crisis simulation with roles, updates, teamwork, final response, and judging.

No. Students need curiosity, teamwork, and willingness to think under pressure. The format rewards leadership, communication, realism, ethics, strategy, and adaptability.

Cabinets are up to three students: Head of State, Finance Minister and Foreign Affairs Minister. A cabinet can run with fewer than three if needed.

Yes. After the Day 1 briefing and strategy block, each team submits an Initial Response Brief. It records the team’s immediate priorities, resource choices, public communication approach, and first action plan before Day 2 consequences are introduced.

Cent$ible

Cent$ible is Élan’s business flagship, run in collaboration with Sanjay Ghodawat International School. It is a live inter-school business arena for students aged 14 to 20 where teams compete and collaborate across two themes, and the strongest teams advance to the Boardroom Finale.

Cent$ible is open to school students aged 14 to 20, across schools and delegations. First-year university students within the same age range are also eligible and can register individually.

It is team-based. Cent$ible is built around two competitive themes, and each theme is its own competition with two rounds. The strongest teams from every theme go through to the Boardroom Finale to compete for the overall championship, and there are networking sessions and interactive activities between rounds.

The two themes are BRAND बनाओ and NOTE नीति. Each theme runs its own rounds and sends its top three teams to the Boardroom Finale.

Cents are Cent$ible’s shared in-event currency. Teams earn, invest, and bid with Cents as the rounds play out.

The Boardroom Finale is the Day 2 round that decides the overall Cent$ible champion. The three highest-scoring teams from every theme come together and are given a business situation drawn from areas such as startup strategy, marketing, finance, brand management, business expansion, crisis management or entrepreneurial decision-making. Teams analyse the information, decide on a course of action, justify it to the board and answer board questioning. It is judged on business knowledge, strategic thinking, creativity, decision-making, problem-solving, financial understanding, communication, confidence, teamwork and composure under pressure.

No specific business background is required. Cent$ible is open to all students aged 14 to 20, and the rounds are built around creativity, strategy, adaptability, communication, and quick thinking.

Registration for the 2026 edition is closed. Cent$ible is not continuing in the second edition.

Yes. The winning team in each flagship receives a ₹10,000 cash prize: ₹10,000 for the winning Crisis Command council and ₹10,000 for the winning Cent$ible team. Prizes are awarded at the Grand Closing and Awards Ceremony, alongside trophies, certificates for every participant, and individual special recognitions.

Tickets

Registration for the 2026 edition is closed. Second-edition registration has not opened.

Venue ticket availability is not guaranteed. Students and schools should register in advance because capacity, badges, and hospitality planning are limited.

Passes are non-refundable. This includes choosing not to attend, illness, travel problems, arriving late, missing a session, or being removed for breaching the Code of Conduct. The one exception is if Élan cancels the event or changes the dates for reasons within our own control. In that case you receive a full refund, or you can move your registration to the new dates. If the event is disrupted by circumstances outside our control, payments are not refunded. Full details are in the Refund Policy.

Transfers are not available as of right. A transfer may be permitted at the Organising Committee’s discretion if requested in writing at least 14 days before the event, and only before portal accounts and team allocations have been issued. The replacement participant must meet the eligibility criteria and complete registration and consent in full. See the Refund Policy for full details.

Confirmation can be sent through email, phone, or official coordinator communication once the registration process is finalized.

Venue

Élan is planned at SGIS Pune Campus, Mulshi, Pune, Maharashtra. Venue access, reporting gates, and room allocations will be shared with confirmed students and school coordinators.

Standard student and delegation passes include access to event-day refreshments. Premium and VIP passes include enhanced hospitality where available.

It depends on your pass. The Standard and Late Registration passes include accommodation for the night of 5 September and transport in Pune between your accommodation and the venue. The Day Delegate pass (₹2,800) does not include accommodation or transport. It is for participants making their own arrangements. Travel to Pune itself is your own arrangement on every pass, though participants coming from Kolhapur can travel with the Élan team at an additional charge. Where accommodation is included, only one night is covered. Extra nights can be arranged at additional cost if you tell us well in advance.
