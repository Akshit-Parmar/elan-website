02 / THE PURPOSE

What participants
actually do.

Participants work in teams, develop proposals and present their decisions.

At the first Élan Summit, students formed cabinets, worked through business challenges and presented decisions they had made together. Each competition required teams to explain their proposals and answer questions.

Outside the committee rooms, there were performances, conversations and a DJ night. The first-edition programme combined competition sessions with these shared activities.

OUR MISSION

Make leadership, creativity and collaboration something young people can practise together.

Can I come if I’ve never done this before?

Yes. You don’t need debate or summit experience. In Crisis Command, you work through a problem with your cabinet. Asking a good question or spotting something your teammates missed is a way to contribute.
