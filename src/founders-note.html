ABOUT ÉLAN / A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

Why I started Élan.

Akshit Parmar
Founder of Élan
Director, 2026 Summit

Jacinth and I were walking back from lunch when we started talking about an event we could build ourselves. I didn’t know then what the opening ceremony would look like, or what it would take to get everyone into the same room. I just knew I wanted to find out.

Élan was my dream project. It became possible because Sakshi, Prachi, Jacinth and the organising committee made it theirs too. As director, I saw how much of their work happened long before a participant walked through the doors. Their names belong in the story as much as mine does.

If you’re thinking about coming to Élan and wondering whether you’ll know what to say, that’s exactly why I want you here. Bring a question. Listen to someone whose answer surprises you. You don’t have to be the loudest person at the table to change the conversation.

I hope we’ll see you at the next one.

Akshit
