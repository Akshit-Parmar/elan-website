A letter from the founder

Dear future delegate,

On the walk back from lunch one day, Jacinth and I got talking about an event we could build ourselves. We wanted students to make the decisions: what happened in the room, who got a turn to speak, and what to do when a plan didn’t work. That conversation was the beginning of Élan.

By the time we opened the doors in Mulshi, the summit belonged to a lot more people than me. Sakshi, Prachi and Jacinth helped found it. Our organising committee did the work that made it possible for everyone else to walk in and take a seat. I’m proud to have my name alongside theirs.

If you’re coming for the first time, I hope you find someone whose answer is different from yours. Ask them how they got there. That’s the conversation I’d want you to remember on your own walk back.

See you at Élan,Akshit
