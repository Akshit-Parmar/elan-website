INSIDE CRISIS COMMAND

Three perspectives.
One cabinet.

A scenario unfolds. Your team weighs its options, agrees on a response and explains its decisions. New developments may ask you to rethink the plan.

01 / HEAD OF STATE

Bring the team together.

Set priorities, hear each minister’s view and present the cabinet’s decisions.

02 / FINANCE MINISTER

Make the numbers work.

Work out what your country can afford and where its resources will make the greatest difference.

03 / FOREIGN AFFAIRS MINISTER

Keep the conversation open.

Negotiate with other countries and consider how each decision affects your relationships.
