The purpose of Élan

A seat at the table.

At Élan, students help decide what happens next. That’s true on the organising committee and in the council room.

01 Our mission

Give students real decisions to make.

In Crisis Command, one cabinet has to decide how to spend a limited budget. Funding one proposal can mean setting another aside. Students have to hear each other out, choose together and explain their decision.

02 Our vision

Make room for the next voice.

We want a student arriving alone from a new school to have as much of a say as someone who already knows the room. The measure of Élan is whether both leave ready to offer an idea, listen to a challenge and invite someone else into the conversation.
