ABOUT ÉLAN / MISSION & VISION

What we want
for students.

Our mission

Give students a place to practise making decisions together. At Élan, you can take responsibility for a cabinet, defend a proposal or help organise the summit itself. You get to see what happens when someone else challenges your idea and you have to decide what to do next.

Our vision

We want students from different schools to feel that they have a place in the same room, whether they love speaking in front of a crowd or are still finding the courage to raise a hand. Our hope is that they take that confidence back to their own classrooms and make room for someone else to speak.
