INSIDE CRISIS COMMAND

What will you actually do?

01 / UNDERSTAND

Read the situation.

Your cabinet represents a country facing a crisis. Work out what has happened, what information is missing and which decisions cannot wait.

02 / DECIDE

Make a case with your team.

Consider competing priorities, discuss resources and agree a response. Explain your reasoning and listen when another member challenges it.

03 / RESPOND

Adapt as events change.

New information can change your plan. Decide what to keep, what to revise and how to explain your next move.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

A flood closes a major transport route.

Communities need supplies, the budget is limited and reports are incomplete. Which places receive help first? What would make you change that decision?

This is an example of the decisions involved, not an announced competition brief. Final rules for the next edition will be published separately.
